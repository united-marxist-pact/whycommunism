In this text, Marx presents a correction on Lassalle's proposed Programme, correcting the phrases while giving an explanation about the stages of economic maturity of Communism wherein quantitative developments in a variety of factors within a classless (and thereby stateless) and moneyless society that develops out of the internal class struggle engendered by Capitalism leads to the positive transcendence beyond Bourgeois Right, that is to say, the Higher Phase of Communism.

These are distinct characteristics and Marx is actually explicit about this to some degree with the understanding of the distinction between the Gemeinwesen, which we'll talk about later most pertinently with Engels, and the Gemeinschaft, which is community as association. That is not to say that these are distinct modes of production; it's in the exact same way as capitalism reaches a higher phase, or stage, imperialism, through a coalition and coagulation of the varying forces within it, leading into financialization and into monopoly capital as the various national markets within each nation get filled up, and then the capitalists must expand internationally and open up markets in foreign nations in competition with foreign bourgeois who want to maintain control over their own national markets and therefore enter imperial warfare, and imperialism itself. To begin with, Marx specifies that Nature is a source of Use Values and is what fundamentally makes up all material wealth (although it must be rendered through human labor to be useful for humans). Therefore, those who claim dominion over the instruments and subjects of labor, such as nature and other such means of production, also claim dominion over the wealth made from that human labor upon them. In such a condition wherein private ownership is in place, Nature becomes mystified, with Labor appearing as the source of Use Values and thereby Wealth in this inversion of social production of wealth. Through the creation of this mystification, and making Labor seem to be above the instruments that Labor is applied to, it makes it so those who possess no property other than their own labor become the slave of those who own the material conditions of labor, as they can not engage in labor without their permission, and thus can not live without their permission. It is only the Labor that the owner demands which is socially recognized, and it is only in social practice that Labor can produce wealth and culture, in and through society (though Labor can produce for the individual and their needs in theory; certainly not what is socially necessary at this stage of historic development).

spoken asideI can't make a phone in my household. I can't make a phone in the middle of nowhere outside of society. I can't make the things that are necessary for modern living within this mode of production.

Next, Marx rejects individualistic accounts of Communism, wherein the "undiminished proceeds of labor" are granted to each individual worker. He specifies that the conception of "proceeds of labor" will eventually disappear as the economic maturity of Communism develops. All societies must reproduce themselves materially to maintain their existence no matter what, being economic necessities that are determined not by equity but rather by available means and forces of production to a given society, and statistics, allowing them to compute probabilities. The greater the understanding and development of these, the more precisely a given society can determine its necessary costs to maintain itself. In all societies, the co-operative proceeds of labor, or the total social product, must be deducted by costs to:

  1. 1. Replacement of Means of Production Used Up (Statistics applied to these would be in the form of Depreciation)
  2. spoken asideIf we're talking in accounting terms.
  3. 2. Expansion of Production (which is determined by a variety of potential and definite factors, e.g. a society might decide to employ greater focus on development of Productive Forces and Producers in some process. The greater the concentration of Means of Production, the more costs are used to replace them too)
  4. spoken asideAnd how a society determines what means of production to expand is definitely determined, but also socially determined. For example, in capitalism, this is determined by the market, and investment of money capital. In communist society, this is determined directly and socially.
  5. 3. Reserve or Insurance funds for accidents, crises, etc

Then, after that, from the so-called "proceeds of labor" further is deducted by all societies, but particularly in Communist society:

  1. 1. General costs of Administration, which will be handled socially through the new Gemeinwesen, the General Intellect of scientific society, which administers things and directs processes of production. The costs of this will REDUCE as Communism economically matures
  2. 2. Common Satisfaction of Needs, which will INCREASE in amount as Communism economically emerges and more is brought into the social sphere (e.g. such a communal society will recognize the benefits of mass social projects, providing all necessities and access to all necessary support to develop the individual to their maximum ability)
  3. 3. Funds for those Unable to Work for whatever reason

Eventually, costs to Expand Production, Replace Current Means of Production, and the development of Common Satisfaction of Needs will use up all available "proceeds of labor", but this thrusts all of humanity into a realm of absolute social wealth: "what the producer is deprived of in his capacity as a private individual comes back to him as a member of society"; all have access to the whole of humanity's collective wealth. Products of society that the individual consumes for themself, e.g. food, products that are used up in daily life, etc., are part of the "To each according to their need" which ever increases as the wealth of society increases. Anything outside of such Means of Consumption are socially available, with all wealth being made social over time with any right of exclusion brought to an end, thus making all products of society commonly held and therefore accessible.

spoken asideSo there's no purpose or use for any sort of hoarding either, to be clear.

Next, Marx explains the distinction between the Lower Phase of Communism, wherein Classes and Money have been abolished, but its economic maturity keeps it unable to fully transcend Bourgeois Right. In Communist Society, a cooperative one based on common ownership of the Means of Production, the associated producers do not exchange their products and Individual Labor is no longer an indirect contribution, with labor time being made abstract and under the ownership of an individual who owns the means by which that labor is carried out. Rather, it is a direct component of the total labor of society. In the Lower Phase, as Communist society is developing, it has the birthmarks of Capitalist society in all aspects. As a result, the individual producers at this stage must receive individual recognition for their individual quantum of labor of that total social labor in the form of some receipt, certificate, voucher, ledger, or any other form of method of tracking individual contribution which evidences they have worked a number of hours of labor after aforementioned deductions.

Using this certificate, the laborer is able to draw from social stock means of consumption that is the same in labor time: "The same amount of labor which he has given in one form, he receives back in another". One time unit (e.g. an Hour) of Socially Necessary Labor Time (Abstracted, Homogenized Human Labor, though negatively recognized as direct component) is equal to itself, In Kind calculations. Thus, there is still some degree of alienation through the abstraction of labor and exchange of equal values, but it is in a new form and content, and is being steadily removed due to their transitory nature. In such a system, individual labor is no longer an indirect contribution to total social labor mediated by Commodity exchange via the labor market, but is instead a direct component of it with its social character being recognized from the onset rather than post-hoc through the market. In addition, no one can give anything except their personal labor rather than sell capital, land, or labor-power, and only individual means of consumption can be drawn from social stock; no one can gain private control over the conditions of production.

As a concrete example of what this would potentially look like from the perspective of someone in a Communist society in the Lower Phase:

There is a warehouse, either a single one for an area or a number of them with specialty products, wherein there are means of subsistence of all kinds available. You either pick them up or potentially have them delivered. This place has no shop owners, and no one is selling the products. Instead, you simply take what you need off of the shelves or ask for what you need from the “accountant”. Some of it will be “free”- society will have been able to be so productive with it, for example basic foodstuffs, that there is no point in individually comparing labor with it, or perhaps it is a Common Good which will be produced regardless of if people will consume it or not, or which society made the decision to include in automatic accounting like healthcare, aloud or like a train, or something along those lines so it is simply included in the aforementioned Common Deductions.

After receiving your personal means of subsistence, you present your labor card which has recorded the quantity of social labor you have done as an individual as a ledger. They then cancel out that period, with one hour of socially necessary labor time being equal to 1 hour of socially necessary labor time in products. The people doing the labor at the warehouse, directing consumers around, inputting information, etc do not receive any vouchers from the consumers- the vouchers do not exchange or persist across the “exchange”; they are destroyed. Instead, they receive their own for the labor time they have done. The purpose of this exchange is not to accrue wealth, but instead to aid in economic planning and deal with remnant social and economic necessities. The warehouses’ stock is replenished according to rational planned economic determinations carried out by the free and equal associated producers, with necessities and common goods initially automatically accounted for and requiring nothing but accounting activity for planning purposes, with the tallied up consumption intended to reach balanced accounts while reporting to the planning bureau so that planning is refined by us knowing what is popular, how much is being consumed, what is unpopular, etc with small caveats for different conditions, such as need for privacy, transportation of products, specialty and unique production, etc.

Where there is overproduction due to shifting demand, you might see some small decrease in “price” due to depreciation and the like, and vice-a-versa with underproduction when statistical anticipation of demand is not in tune with production, with the true labor time being printed on them.

This Lower Phase with Labor Certification is fundamentally within Bourgeois limitations, with the right of producers being proportional to the labor they supply, thus barring people from access to the whole of society's wealth.

spoken asideInstead they have their individual wealth as individual producers.

The equality within this society is in that measurement is made with the equal standard that is labor time within that society, which comes with further caveats:

  1. 1. All those outside of that given society in terms of economic maturity are thereby unequal in relation and are in deficit in comparison
  2. 2. Because Labor acts as the measure, it must be defined by duration and intensity

Therefore, for equality to be the case, inequality must be negatively transcended through force of will: labor must be treated unequally, with unequal individuals granted more or less, and given individual remuneration more or less, according to their ability. Right in this society is unequal and the only way to reduce this is effective Affirmative Action nationally and internationally to make labor equal. Such is only possible as society develops and makes it possible though.

spoken asideThis is where something like the Joint Dictatorship of the Proletariat is needed, wherein all the different nations that are part of the Dictatorship of the Proletariat, and then later within the world republic, combine their economic might as one. And different regions that are less developed, they of course require additional help. And regions that are highly developed require less help. So there's an equalization process, and some nations will undergo stricter conditions. Let's just put it like that. But there will be no costs to any individuals, to be clear. It is on a societal basis that we're looking at this. Continuing. Eventually, this is… oh, and also this supports people who are unable to work for any given reason, be it age, or disability, or physical accident, or anything along those lines, right? So…

Eventually, this is positively transcended as the economic structure of society develops with the tempered cultural development expanding; this is the point at which Bourgeois Right is escaped, that is to say that the Higher Phase of Communism is achieved and the condition of "From Each According to their Ability, to Each According to their Needs!" This state is realized as:

  1. 1. The Division of Labor is ended
  2. 2. Labor becomes life's prime want as all Alienation ceases
  3. 3. The Productive Forces and all Individuals have reached high development for co-operative wealth being made fully accessible

At this point, no right of inequality is necessary, or recognition of individual labor; all that is left is calculation of probabilities, administration of production, and the resultant organization of distribution which, in this case, is determined by the material conditions of production being the co-operative property of the freely associated producers, thus resulting in an entirely new form of distribution. Labor certification becomes needed only for purposes of planning and on a social basis, not individual. More and more means of subsistence gets produced to the point where it becomes a fetter on society to demand individual payment for it- "we've got more potatoes than we know what to do with, just take them, it's already accounted for" as an example. This expands more and more, and more of social production is brought together. Individual claims become a fetter on production and consumption as a right of exclusion: the desire for greater wealth and leisure within Communism leads people to abolishing remaining bourgeois conceptions of property. In addition, society is geared towards Common Goods, e.g. trains and buses over private vehicles’ ultimately, the Common Wealth, or the gennosenschaftlicher Reichtum, emerges in full. This is not unlimited access to abundant goods, but rather a reversal of primitive accumulation's artificial scarcity with radical abundance in the form of the common wealth which makes social wealth equally accessible to everyone at the cost of private wealth. It is the commonification of wealth, rehabilitating it into abundance.

The great significance of Marx's explanation is, that here too, he consistently applies materialist dialectics, the theory of development, and regards communism as something which develops out of capitalism. Instead of scholastically invented, 'concocted' definitions and fruitless disputes over words (What is socialism? What is communism?), Marx gives analysis of what might be called the stages of the economic maturity of communism.

  1. Marx presents Communism as something that develops out of Capitalism
  2. He expresses stages of economic maturity of Communism

Labor is not the source of all wealth. Nature is just as much the source of use values (and it is surely of such that material wealth consists!) as labor, which itself is only the manifestation of a force of nature, human labor power. the above phrase is to be found in all children's primers and is correct insofar as it is implied that labor is performed with the appurtenant subjects and instruments. But a socialist program cannot allow such bourgeois phrases to pass over in silence the conditions that lone give them meaning. And insofar as man from the beginning behaves toward nature, the primary source of all instruments and subjects of labor, as an owner, treats her as belonging to him, his labor becomes the source of use values, therefore also of wealth. The bourgeois have very good grounds for falsely ascribing supernatural creative power to labor; since precisely from the fact that labor depends on nature it follows that the man who possesses no other property than his labor power must, in all conditions of society and culture, be the slave of other men who have made themselves the owners of the material conditions of labor. He can only work with their permission, hence live only with their permission.

  1. Nature is a source of use values and is what makes up material wealth, though it must be rendered through human labor to be useful for humans
  2. Those who claim dominion over the instruments and subjects of labor, such as nature and other such means of production, also claim dominion over the products made from that human labor upon them
  3. Labor becomes the source of use values rather than Nature, and therefore Labor also becomes the source of wealth in this mystifying inversion of social production of wealth
  4. By creating this mystification and making Labor seem to be above the instruments than Labor is applied to, it makes it so that those who possess no property other than their own labor become the slave of those who own the material conditions of labor, as they can not engage in labor without their permission, and thus cannot live without their permission

Labor becomes the source of wealth and culture only as social labor", or, what is the same thing, "in and through society".

This proposition is incontestably correct, for although isolated labor (its material conditions presupposed) can create use value, it can create neither wealth nor culture.

But equally incontestable is this other proposition:

"In proportion as labor develops socially, and becomes thereby a source of wealth and culture, poverty and destitution develop among the workers, and wealth and culture among the nonworkers."

This is the law of all history hitherto. What, therefore, had to be done here, instead of setting down general phrases about "labor" and "society", was to prove concretely how in present capitalist society the material, etc., conditions have at last been created which enable and compel the workers to lift this social curse.

  1. Labor can produce for the individual their needs, but it cannot produce wealth or culture except in and through society

Let us take, first of all, the words "proceeds of labor" in the sense of the product of labor; then the co-operative proceeds of labor are the total social product.

From this must now be deducted:

1. First, cover for replacement of the means of production used up.

2. Second, additional portion for expansion of production.

3. Third, reserve or insurance funds to provide against accidents, dislocations caused by natural calamities, etc.

These deductions from the "undiminished" proceeds of labor are an economic necessity, and their magnitude is to be determined according to available means and forces, and partly by computation of probabilities, but they are in no way calculable by equity.

There remains the other part of the total product, intended to serve as means of consumption.

Before this is divided among the individuals, there has to be deducted again, from it:

1. First, the general costs of administration not belonging to production. This part will, from the outset, be very considerably restricted in comparison with present-day society, and it diminishes in proportion as the new society develops.

2. Second, that which is intended for the common satisfaction of needs, such as schools, health services, etc. From the outset, this part grows considerably in comparison with present-day society, and it grows in proportion as the new society develops.

3. Third, funds for those unable to work, etc., in short, for what is included under so-called official poor relief today.

Only now do we come to the "distribution" which the program, under Lassallean influence, alone has in view in its narrow fashion -- namely, to that part of the means of consumption which is divided among the individual producers of the co-operative society.

The "undiminished" proceeds of labor have already unnoticeably become converted into the "diminished" proceeds, although what the producer is deprived of in his capacity as a private individual benefits him directly or indirectly in his capacity as a member of society.

Just as the phrase of the "undiminished" proceeds of labor has disappeared, so now does the phrase of the "proceeds of labor" disappear altogether.

  1. In the Communist Mode of Production, the conception of the "proceeds of labor" will eventually disappear as the economic maturity of Communism develops. All societies must reproduce themselves, in a number of basic points in order to maintain their existence. Such are economic necessities, the amount deducted determined by not by equity, but by
    1. Available means and forces
    2. Statistics, ability to compute Probabilities
  2. The Co-operative proceeds of labor are the total social product, deducted by costs to:
    1. Replacement of Means of Production used up
    2. Expansion of Production (determined by a variety of potential and definite factors)
    3. Reverse or Insurance funds for accidents, crises, etc
  3. For the part of Means of Consumption in the total social product, the "proceeds of labor" will be further deducted:
    1. General costs of Administration, which will be handled socially, through the new Gemeinwesen, the General Intellect of Scientific Society, which administers things and directs processes of production. The costs of this will REDUCE as Communist economically matures
    2. Common Satisfaction of Needs, which will increase in amount as Communism economically matures and more is brought into the social sphere
    3. Funds for those Unable to Work for whatever reason
  4. Eventually, costs to expand production and common satisfaction of needs will use up all available "proceeds of labor", but this thrusts all of humanity into a realm of absolute social wealth: what the producers is deprived of in his capacity as a private individual comes back to him as a member of society; all have access to all of humanity's collective wealth
  5. Products of society that you consume for yourself, e.g. food, stuff you use in your daily life, etc is under the "to each according to their need". Anything outside of that is socially available. All wealth is made social over time. Private Property as a right of exclusion comes to an end and with it, all products of society are commonly held and therefore accessible

Within the co-operative society based on common ownership of the means of production, the producers do not exchange their products; just as little does the labor employed on the products appear here as the value of these products, as a material quality possessed by them, since now, in contrast to capitalist society, individual labor no longer exists in an indirect fashion but directly as a component part of total labor. The phrase "proceeds of labor", objectionable also today on account of its ambiguity, thus loses all meaning

  1. In a cooperative society based on common ownership of the Means of Production, the associated producers do not exchange their products
  2. Individual labor no longer is an indirect contribution, with labor time being made abstract and under the ownership of an individual who owns the means by which that labor is carried out. Instead, it is a direct component part of total labor

What we have to deal with here is a communist society, not as it has developed on its own foundations, but, on the contrary, just as it emerges from capitalist society; which is thus in every respect, economically, morally, and intellectually, still stamped with the birthmarks of the old society from whose womb it emerges. Accordingly, the individual producer receives back from society -- after the deductions have been made -- exactly what he gives to it. What he has given to it is his individual quantum of labor. For example, the social working day consists of the sum of the individual hours of work; the individual labor time of the individual producer is the part of the social working day contributed by him, his share in it. He receives a certificate from society that he has furnished such-and-such an amount of labor (after deducting his labor for the common funds); and with this certificate, he draws from the social stock of means of consumption as much as the same amount of labor cost. The same amount of labor which he has given to society in one form, he receives back in another.

  1. In the Lower Phase, as Communist society has not yet reached economic maturity, has the birthmarks of Capitalist society in all aspects. As a result, for the time being, the individual producers must receive individual recognition for their individual quantum of labor
  2. This would involve some form of certificate from society that evidences that they have worked a number of hours of labor after aforementioned deductions
  3. Using this certificate, they are able to draw from social stock means of consumption that is the same in labor time: "The same amount of labor which he has given to society in one form, he receives back in another."

Here, obviously, the same principle prevails as that which regulates the exchange of commodities, as far as this is exchange of equal values. Content and form are changed, because under the altered circumstances no one can give anything except his labor, and because, on the other hand, nothing can pass to the ownership of individuals, except individual means of consumption. But as far as the distribution of the latter among the individual producers is concerned, the same principle prevails as in the exchange of commodity equivalents: a given amount of labor in one form is exchanged for an equal amount of labor in another form.

  1. With these certificates, or vouchers, or ledger, or any other form of tracking individual contribution where a Time Unit of Socially Necessary Labor Time (Abstracted, Homogenized Human Labor) = Time Unit of Socially Necessary Labor Time (Abstracted, Homogenized Human Labor.
  2. While Labor acts as the common standard, it does not get exchanged, and its content and form changes as no one can give anything except their personal labor, and only individual means of consumption can be drawn from social stock; no one can gain private control over the conditions of production.

In spite of this advance, this equal right is still constantly stigmatized by a bourgeois limitation. The right of the producers is proportional to the labor they supply; the equality consists in the fact that measurement is made with an equal standard, labor.

But one man is superior to another physically, or mentally, and supplies more labor in the same time, or can labor for a longer time; and labor, to serve as a measure, must be defined by its duration or intensity, otherwise it ceases to be a standard of measurement. This equal right is an unequal right for unequal labor. It recognizes no class differences, because everyone is only a worker like everyone else; but it tacitly recognizes unequal individual endowment, and thus productive capacity, as a natural privilege. It is, therefore, a right of inequality, in its content, like every right. Right, by its very nature, can consist only in the application of an equal standard; but unequal individuals (and they would not be different individuals if they were not unequal) are measurable only by an equal standard insofar as they are brought under an equal point of view, are taken from one definite side only -- for instance, in the present case, are regarded only as workers and nothing more is seen in them, everything else being ignored. Further, one worker is married, another is not; one has more children than another, and so on and so forth. Thus, with an equal performance of labor, and hence an equal in the social consumption fund, one will in fact receive more than another, one will be richer than another, and so on. To avoid all these defects, right, instead of being equal, would have to be unequal.

  1. This Lower Phase with Labor Certification is fundamentally within bourgeois limitations, with the right of producers being proportional to the labor they supply, thus barring people from access to the whole of society's wealth
  2. The equality within this society is in that measurement is made with the equal standard that is labor time within that society. Note:
    1. All those outside of that give society in terms of economic maturity are thereby unequal in relation
    2. In addition, because labor acts as the measure, it is defined by its duration and intensity
    3. Therefore, for equality to be the case, labor must be treated unequally, with unequal individuals granted more or less, and given individual remuneration more or less, according to their ability. Right in this society is unequal; Affirmative Action would have to be put in place nationally and internationally to make labor equal, and this is only possible as society develops

But these defects are inevitable in the first phase of communist society as it is when it has just emerged after prolonged birth pangs from capitalist society. Right can never be higher than the economic structure of society and its cultural development conditioned thereby.

  1. Eventually, this is positively transcended as the economic structure of society develops and the cultural development is expanded.

In a higher phase of communist society, after

1. the enslaving subordination of the individual to the division of labor, and therewith also the antithesis between mental and physical labor, has vanished;

2. after labor has become not only a means of life but life's prime want;

3. after the productive forces have also increased with the all-around development of the individual, and

4. all the springs of co-operative wealth flow more abundantly --

only then then can the narrow horizon of bourgeois right be crossed in its entirety and society inscribe on its banners: From each according to his ability, to each according to his needs!

  1. The Higher Phase of Communism is achieved once bourgeois right can be fully positively transcended, and we can achieve the condition of "From each according to their ability, to each according to their needs!"
  2. This is achieved via the end of the division of labor, the end of alienation with labor becoming life's prime want, and once the productive forces and all individuals have reached high development for co-operative wealth being made fully accessible
  3. No longer is any right of inequality necessary, any recognition of individual labor, etc. Labor certification is only needed on a social basis, not individual

Quite apart from the analysis so far given, it was in general a mistake to make a fuss about so-called distribution and put the principal stress on it.

Any distribution whatever of the means of consumption is only a consequence of the distribution of the conditions of production themselves. The latter distribution, however, is a feature of the mode of production itself. The capitalist mode of production, for example, rests on the fact that the material conditions of production are in the hands of nonworkers in the form of property in capital and land, while the masses are only owners of the personal condition of production, of labor power. If the elements of production are so distributed, then the present-day distribution of the means of consumption results automatically. If the material conditions of production are the co-operative property of the workers themselves, then there likewise results a distribution of the means of consumption different from the present one. Vulgar socialism (and from it in turn a section of the democrats) has taken over from the bourgeois economists the consideration and treatment of distribution as independent of the mode of production and hence the presentation of socialism as turning principally on distribution. After the real relation has long been made clear, why retrogress again?

  1. Distribution of means of consumption is a consequence of the distribution of the conditions of production, a feature of the mode of production. Socialism, therefore, is not determined by distribution
  2. In a Communist society, wherein material conditions of production are the co-operative property of the freely associated producers as opposed to one wherein the products of labor are owned by the private owners of those, then distribution of a different kind will be in play

The question then arises: What transformation will the state undergo in communist society? In other words, what social functions will remain in existence there that are analogous to present state functions? This question can only be answered scientifically, and one does not get a flea-hop nearer to the problem by a thousand-fold combination of the word 'people' with the word 'state'.

Between capitalist and communist society there lies the period of the revolutionary transformation of the one into the other. Corresponding to this is also a political transition period in which the state can be nothing but the revolutionary dictatorship of the proletariat.

  1. In the Dictatorship of the Proletariat, the People become the State
  2. The State will transform in a scientific manner wherein the social functions that remain in existence, but have changed fundamentally with the new relations in society and its method of reproduction, are continually adopted and extracted by other structures until it withers away
