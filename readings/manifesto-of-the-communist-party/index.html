spoken · the reader's introductionWelcome to the fifth video in the series, The Character of the Communist Mode of Production According to Marx and Engels. Today we’ll be going over the 1848 piece by Marx and Engels, Manifesto of the Communist Party. I subtitled it “From Proletarian Rule to Classless Community as Association.”

In this text, in terms of the Communist Mode of Production, Marx lays out the historical development of the Proletariat and the direction their class interests lead them to engaging in class struggle with. From there, he discusses the resultant conditions this will bring about when brought to fruition. It addresses Class Struggle, Labor, Individuals, Nations and the State as a social formation.

spoken · this text in all three seriesAnd to be clear, this text will be reappearing in all three series, because it touches on all three different topics — that is, the Dictatorship of the Proletariat and the Communist Party.

With the seizure of political power, the establishment of a Dictatorship of the Proletariat, the ever greater socialization of labor and the end of competition, new relations develop: Socialist relations. By making itself the new ruling class and destroying the old conditions of production, they in turn negate their own class basis and, in abolishing themselves as the last Class, Class society itself is negated.

In Communism, accumulated labor is specifically with the intent of improving the conditions of the laborers, since the laborers make all the decisions about how labor is organized: living labor dominates dead labor, Dead labor being, for example, machines, and so on and so forth. Living labor being labor that is actually going in and doing stuff right now. Dead labor is termed that because it has been fixed in a particular form. with humans controlling their own instruments of production rather than the other way around. All have access to the products of society, with the only product barred being the power to subjugate the labor of others through the products of society.

The individual is brought to full development in and through society, with the sale of flesh brought to an end, the Patriarchal Family (private prostitution of women) brought to an end, with freedom not bought through exploitation and oppression but through justice and in the real community. Rather than Class Struggle, Communism is the association between producers wherein the basis for the free development of the individual is the condition for the free development of all.

Nations cease having material basis for conflict, and because such basis rests in the world market which has brought all nations into ties of dependence and uniform production, it becomes possible for a world revolution to occur which can achieve liberation for all peoples and establish a world republic. Capitalism has done the work of bringing all nations into one system, and now the workers of the world can fully combine to remove the dominance of some nations over others, competition, etc. and so liberate all and enter into final and fully positive and equal relations.

The State ultimately withers away: public power loses its political character because Class distinctions disappear with all production concentrated in the hands of the whole of society. This loss of political character is because all previously politicized public power, or political power, has only been the power of one class to oppress another: such class-based oppression disappears.

QUOTATION EXTRACTS

The proletariat goes through various stages of development. With its birth begins its struggle with the bourgeoisie. At first the contest is carried on by individual labourers, then by the workpeople of a factory, then by the operative of one trade, in one locality, against the individual bourgeois who directly exploits them. They direct their attacks not against the bourgeois conditions of production, but against the instruments of production themselves; they destroy imported wares that compete with their labour, they smash to pieces machinery, they set factories ablaze, they seek to restore by force the vanished status of the workman of the Middle Ages

  1. The immediate class interests of the Proletariat lead them to attack the means of production, competition with their labor
  2. These immediate class interests are reactionary in character: they seek to return to a previous class relation

In bourgeois society, living labour is but a means to increase accumulated labour. In Communist society, accumulated labour is but a means to widen, to enrich, to promote the existence of the labourer.

In bourgeois society, therefore, the past dominates the present; in Communist society, the present dominates the past.

In bourgeois society capital is independent and has individuality, while the living person is dependent and has no individuality. And the abolition of this state of things is called by the bourgeois, abolition of individuality and freedom! And rightly so. The abolition of bourgeois individuality, bourgeois independence, and bourgeois freedom is undoubtedly aimed at.

  1. In Communism, accumulated labor is specifically with the intent of improving conditions of the laborers
  2. In Communism, Living Labor dominates Dead Labor: humans control their means of production, not the other way around
  3. In Communism, bourgeois individuality, independence and freedom is abolished. In its place, we will have true individuality, independence, and freedom in and through society. Rather than freedom bought by exploitation and oppression, actual freedom can be obtained: justice.

Communism deprives no man of the power to appropriate the products of society; all that it does is to deprive him of the power to subjugate the labour of others by means of such appropriations.

  1. In Communism, all have access to the products of society (as opposed to Capitalism)
  2. In Communism, the only product of society that is barred is the power to subjugate the labor of others through the products of society

For the rest, it is selfevident that the abolition of the present system of production must bring with it the abolition of the community of women springing from that system, i.e., of prostitution both public and private.

  1. In Communism, the sale of flesh will end in all its forms
  2. In Communism, the Family will cease being patriarchal

In proportion as the exploitation of one individual by another will also be put an end to, the exploitation of one nation by another will also be put an end to. In proportion as the antagonism between classes within the nation vanishes, the hostility of one nation to another will come to an end.

  1. In Communism, nations will no longer have material basis for conflicts

National differences and antagonism between peoples are daily more and more vanishing, owing to the development of the bourgeoisie, to freedom of commerce, to the world market, to uniformity in the mode of production and in the conditions of life corresponding thereto.

  1. The basis for this ending material basis for conflicts lies in the world market, which brings all nation into ties of dependence with one another and uniform production. Thus, with the world revolution, we will be able to finally achieve liberation for all peoples and establish a world republic

All the preceding classes that got the upper hand sought to fortify their already acquired status by subjecting society at large to their conditions of appropriation. The proletarians cannot become masters of the productive forces of society, except by abolishing their own previous mode of appropriation, and thereby also every other previous mode of appropriation. They have nothing of their own to secure and to fortify; their mission is to destroy all previous securities for, and insurances of, individual property.

  1. Because of the special character of the Proletariat as the last class, they can only take full power over the means of production by abolishing the bourgeois mode of production and all property relations therein. Anything less does not bring about a new mode of production

When, in the course of development, class distinctions have disappeared, and all production has been concentrated in the hands of a vast association of the whole nation, the public power will lose its political character. Political power, properly so called, is merely the organised power of one class for oppressing another. If the proletariat during its contest with the bourgeoisie is compelled, by the force of circumstances, to organise itself as a class, if, by means of a revolution, it makes itself the ruling class, and, as such, sweeps away by force the old conditions of production, then it will, along with these conditions, have swept away the conditions for the existence of class antagonisms and of classes generally, and will thereby have abolished its own supremacy as a class.

In place of the old bourgeois society, with its classes and class antagonisms, we shall have an association, in which the free development of each is the condition for the free development of all.

  1. In Communism, public power loses its political character because all class distinctions will have disappeared
  2. Previous politicized public power, or political power, has only ever been the power of one class to oppress another
  3. In the course of the revolution, the Proletariat may make itself the ruling class and forcibly destroy the old conditions of production
  4. In the destruction of these old conditions, they remove their own class basis, and as the last class, they in turn remove the existence of any class
  5. In Communism, rather than class struggle, we will have association between producers wherein the basis for free development of the individual is the free development of all
