spoken · the reader's introductionWelcome to the third video in the series: the character of the Communist Mode of Production According to Marx and Engels. Today we’ll be going over my summary of the 1844 piece by Marx: Economic and Philosophic Manuscripts of 1844 — Private Property and Communism. I’ve subtitled this: Communism Achieves Scientific Society, Ends Abstraction and Fetishism.

In this text, Marx presents early conceptions of a transitional period before Communism, the Dictatorship of the Proletariat in the pre-Communist phase. He goes over the character of Private Property in relation to Communism and the results of its abolition; both the process of societal transformation in the lower, crude phase of communism, and its complete negation into the higher phase.

Private Property is positively transcended, as it is abolished and we no longer need to structure society to expropriate it. Mankind becomes free from the Dictatorship of the Proletariat as the Proletariat abolishes itself through its own class interests. From this positive transcendence, human essence is appropriated by man, and for man, with the true community of man being established.

All contradictions become resolved: existence and essence; objectification and self-confirmation; freedom and necessity; individual and species. The riddle of history is thus solved, and consciously at that. With Bourgeois right, which is a right of exclusion, being abolished, the true realm of freedom becomes open for mankind. Labor becomes life's prime want, as labor becomes sensuous passion, which becomes the basis for living and action. Wealth and Poverty receive new human, social, significance, as Wealth (free time, use values) produces rich human beings and thereby needs, with high desires with a totality of manifestations of life and self-actualization, while Poverty (labor being life's prime want) produces the desire for humans to experience the need of the greatest wealth under Communism: other fully developed human beings, or true community.

In Communism, that is the true community of man, mankind returns to himself as a social being through self-consciousness and the embrace of all the wealth of historical development, making all held by all. In turn, a true humanism emerges as naturalism is fully developed, thus producing a scientific society which becomes unable to consider itself separate from nature or claim an alien being above man and nature. This, in turn, resolves the conflict between man and man and man and nature.

This scientific society is populated by scientists, as alienation from society in the form of property, ideology born of property, and divisions of labor influencing the senses, comes to an end: the full character of reality becomes clear to all humans with the Unique becoming clear as abstraction and fetishism is brought to an end. Thereby, all labor and nature becomes recognized for its true, unique character.

In this scientific society, thought proceeds from the "theoretically and practically sensuous consciousness of man and of nature as the essence". As such, Atheism in turn is negated, as atheism is a negation of God: socialism is positive self-consciousness and in a society which no longer must seek to abolish religion, we no longer have to concern ourselves with abolishing it. As such, Atheism ceases. Because theism also ceases.

However, this requires a transitionary period to achieve, and is itself not the end of human development. Communism is the position of negation of the negation, which creates the basis for the next stage of historical development in the process of human emancipation and rehabilitation: Communism is not the end goal, and after it is achieved and all hitherto history is brought to an end through the abolition of classes, a new volume in human history can finally be opened with new driving factors.

Until that negation of the negation is reached, the negatory condition is a transitional period which is the Dictatorship of the Proletariat. This is still political in nature and can either be democratic or despotic so long as it continues to abolish private property. Under this circumstance, the state is not fully abolished as private property and thus alienation remains. However, in such a society, mankind is fully conscious of that alienation. This will lead mankind to abolish Bourgeois Right through the seeking of practical changes in industry, science and relations of production which will allow mankind to transcend bourgeois ideology that keeps them captivated by the idea of private property.

It is at this point, and with the end of all remnants of bourgeois society, that a new epoch of history begins with new forms of emancipation for humanity.

QUOTATION EXTRACTS

The first positive annulment of private property – crude communism – is thus merely a manifestation of the vileness of private property, which wants to set itself up as the positive community system.

  1. Early ideological iterations of Communist thought (e.g. the Diggers) were purely reactionary against private property.
  2. It sets itself as the positive annulment of private property, but enters into a universalization of principle which is confused, e.g. “community of women” (perceiving women as private property)

(2) Communism (α) still political in nature – democratic or despotic; (β) with the abolition of the state, yet still incomplete, and being still affected by private property, i.e., by the estrangement of man. In both forms communism already is aware of being reintegration or return of man to himself, the transcendence of human self-estrangement; but since it has not yet grasped the positive essence of private property, and just as little the human nature of need, it remains captive to it and infected by it. It has, indeed, grasped its concept, but not its essence.

  1. The early conception of Communism still presents a society that is political in nature
  2. Crude Communism still has Private property still affecting the state and produces alienation
  3. Both the lower and higher phase begin the process of ending alienation
  4. Eventually, as society changes, people will let go of remaining bourgeois ideology that keeps them captivated by the idea of private property

(3) Communism as the positive transcendence of private property as human self-estrangement, and therefore as the real appropriation of the human essence by and for man; communism therefore as the complete return of man to himself as a social (i.e., human) being – a return accomplished consciously and embracing the entire wealth of previous development. This communism, as fully developed naturalism, equals humanism, and as fully developed humanism equals naturalism; it is the genuine resolution of the conflict between man and nature and between man and man – the true resolution of the strife between existence and essence, between objectification and self-confirmation, between freedom and necessity, between the individual and the species. Communism is the riddle of history solved, and it knows itself to be this solution.

  1. In Communism, Private Property is positively transcended, as it is abolished and we no longer need to structure society to expropriate it
  2. From this positive transcendence, human essence is appropriated by man, and for man, with the true community of man being established
  3. Mankind becomes free from the Dictatorship of the Proletariat as the Proletariat abolishes itself through its own class interests
  4. All contradictions become resolved: existence and essence; objectification and self-confirmation; freedom and necessity; individual and species. The riddle of history is thus solved, and consciously at that

    But since for the socialist man the entire so-called history of the world is nothing but the creation of man through human labour, nothing but the emergence of nature for man, so he has the visible, irrefutable proof of his birth through himself, of his genesis. Since the real existence of man and nature has become evident in practice, through sense experience, because man has thus become evident for man as the being of nature, and nature for man as the being of man, the question about an alien being, about a being above nature and man – a question which implies the admission of the unreality of nature and of man – has become impossible in practice. Atheism, as the denial of this unreality, has no longer any meaning, for atheism is a negation of God, and postulates the existence of man through this negation; but socialism as socialism no longer stands in any need of such a mediation.
