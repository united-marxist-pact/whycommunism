WHY AREBIRTH RATESDECLINING?

by modjuicer

▾

Well...

probably the fact that as a culture we no longer see children as useful, or as productive members of society, neither do we do much in the way of childcare, or raising children in communities. Instead we see them as expensive, high maintenance investments into the future that not all can afford. Instead of providing even a fleeting opportunity to prove their worth to those around them or take initiative on things, we sanction off their most critical areas of development as time for them to wake up every day much earlier than when is natural or healthy for their age, according to strong anthropological data on sleep patterns, for them to sit at a desk doing busy work and listening to boring lectures for such long periods of time that it creates mental health problems beyond just their sleep, all so we can fill them full of facts and information of dubious value to their future within a poorly integrated curriculum of education which is not formulated with considerations for its full applicability towards the end goal of preparing students to succeed later in life, or make society better off. A curriculum which itself is largely determined by closed off, monopolistic textbook publishers and is administered by a system which provides no tangible compensation to the students for enduring its mandatory education regimen.

Children aren’t easy to take care of if you don’t have a larger community, as the lack of community leads to the over-concentration of parental responsibilities on single individuals as well as a complete lack of flexibility that comes with never having a backup to rely on, which makes both the parenting and childhood experience worse, and restricts social opportunities for children during their most critical areas of social development, which causes higher rates of depression and suicide amongst children, and as the social environment is so sparse, it leaves them subject almost entirely to the tendencies and dispositions of their parents.

long story short -

children don’t play as much of a role in society as they should,

childcare is privatized,

community is fractured,

opportunities are restricted,

and as a result the notion of being either parent or child has become increasingly unappealing, and anti-natalist mentalities have gained an increasing level of traction among people.
