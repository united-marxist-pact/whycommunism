About

United Marxist Pact (UMP) is a vibrant community of Communists dedicated to learning, discussing, and applying dialectical materialism to change the world. We strive to create a safe and inclusive space for comrades of all backgrounds, identities, and experiences.

What to expect

  • 📚 Resources & Discussion. Explore our resources, engage in discussions, and connect with comrades worldwide.
  • 🌍 International Comrades. Join a global community of communists and revolutionaries united in struggle.
  • ⚖ Safe, Principled Space. Our moderation team maintains a safe, respectful environment, free from toxicity and bigotry.
  • 🌈 Inclusive & Anti-Oppression. We celebrate diversity, promote inclusivity, and stand against intersecting forms of oppression.
  • 💯 Theory united with praxis.

Our principles

  • We seek to build a true human community, support the emancipation of the proletariat, and transform our social relations and identity.
  • Doing this within a community that pre-figures communist society, while maintaining principled internationalism.
  • We aim to lay the foundations for economic planning, ecological sustainability, and a new social metabolism.
  • We oppose sectarianism, dogmatism, opportunism, revisionism, social chauvinism, class collaborationism, populism, social democracy, patriarchy, reformism, and all other corrupting influences that act upon the proletarian movement.
  • We strive to establish a correct and Principled Movement through a programme which guides correct tactics, with a cybernetically organized system that brings about centralization, discipline, and adherence to our principles.

Join the United Marxist Pact today and become part of our work to create a highly developed organ of Proletarian education!
